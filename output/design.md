# Design notes (Problem 10)

## What changed
- Yale-blue navy header and gold accent on a warm parchment/sky gradient (not a flat Vite default).
- Libre Baskerville for brand/headlines + Source Sans 3 for UI text.
- Full-bleed hero with brand-first “Campus Customs” mark before the headline.
- Product cards with light motion (lift + image scale) and a pulsing chat FAB so help is discoverable.
- Detail pages show a stock board beside the large product image.

## Why it helps customers buy
Clear campus branding builds trust that this is the Yale merch desk; readable type and honest stock on the detail page reduce bounce when someone is size-checking; the chat affordance stays visible without covering product images.
